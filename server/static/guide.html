1. How to Translate Video Subtitles (Anime)

Auto-Translate & Inject

When watching anime or videos with English subtitles, the extension can translate and overlay them instantly in Khmer:

  1. Open the video streaming page (e.g., Anistream, Khanime, or KHFullHD) and start playing the video.
  2. Open the extension popup. If player subtitles are found, you will see a green "English Subtitles Detected!" notification.
  3. Click "Auto Translate & Inject". The AI will translate the lines and overlay them onto the video player.

Translate & Inject Local Subtitle Files

You can also translate and play local subtitle files from your computer:

  1. Drag & drop a subtitle file (.srt, .vtt, .ass, or .lrc) into the upload area in the popup.
  2. Select your Source Language and Target Language (Khmer).
  3. Click "Translate & Inject" to run translation and display them on the video player immediately.
  4. Or, click "Inject Only" to play your subtitles without running any translation.
  5. Once completed, you can click "Save / Download" to keep the translated subtitle file on your disk.

2. How to Translate Manga Pages

Instant Visual Manga Translation

Read foreign manga pages in clean Khmer. The AI reads the original text bubbles, clears them, and draws Khmer text in their place:

  1. Open a manga chapter on a supported site (e.g., MangaKatana).
  2. Click the extension icon. You will see a "Manga Detected!" card showing the manga title, chapter, and total pages.
  3. Select your preferred Manga Translation Mode in the settings tab (see below) and click "Auto Translate Manga".
  4. The extension will process each page, replacing the original text bubbles with Khmer translations automatically.

Manga Translation Modes

  • Normal Mode (Precise Overlay): Removes the original English/Japanese text and redraws Khmer translations exactly inside the speech bubbles. (Requires the Manga Server to be active).
  • Fast Mode (Side-by-Side): Displays translations instantly without redraws, offering a quick reading flow.

3. Supported Websites

The extension automatically integrates and runs on the following platforms:

  • Anime & Video Streaming: Anistream, Khanime, KHFullHD
  • Manga & Comics: MangaKatana, Comix.to, MangaPlus

4. Settings & Configurations Explained

Gemini API Key Required for AI

Your personal connection key from Google AI Studio. It is completely free, does not require a credit card, and ensures your translation requests go directly and securely from your browser to Google Gemini.

Select Model Optional

Choose the AI model brain. We recommend gemini-3.1-flash-lite for subtitles (very fast and uses less quota) and gemini-3.5-flash for manga.

Manga Server URL Required for Normal Mode

The address of your image-clearing server (e.g. https://khdownloader.xyz). This server removes original text from manga panels so the extension can draw Khmer text in empty bubbles.

Smart Local Cache Optional

Saves all translated dialogue locally in your browser. Replaying a video or re-reading a manga chapter loads translations instantly from your local disk without consuming any API keys.

Mature Content (18+) Optional

Allows the AI to translate slang, swearing, and adult/violent dialogue accurately without censoring or triggering safety blocks.

1. 如何翻译视频字幕 (动漫)

自动翻译与注入

当您在观看带有英文或日文字幕的动漫视频时，本扩展可以立即将其翻译并以高精度的柬埔寨语（高棉语）覆写到播放器中：

  1. 打开视频播放页面（例如 Anistream、Khanime 或 KHFullHD）并开始播放视频。
  2. 打开扩展程序弹窗。如果检测到播放器字幕，您将看到绿色的 "English Subtitles Detected!"（检测到英文旁白字幕） 提示。
  3. 点击 "Auto Translate & Inject"（自动翻译并注入）。AI 将执行翻译并自动在视频上展示高棉语字幕。

翻译并注入本地字幕文件

您也可以翻译并播放您电脑中的本地字幕文件：

  1. 将字幕文件（.srt、.vtt、.ass 或 .lrc）拖拽到弹窗中的上传区域。
  2. 选择 源语言 与 目标语言（柬埔寨高棉语）。
  3. 点击 "Translate & Inject" 运行 AI 翻译并立即展示在播放器上。
  4. 或者，点击 "Inject Only" 仅在播放器上加载字幕而不进行翻译。
  5. 翻译完成后，您可以点击 "Save / Download" 将翻译好的字幕文件保存到您的电脑中。

2. 如何翻译漫画图片

网页漫画即时翻译

直接在网页中阅读高棉语漫画。AI 会智能识别漫画对白、清除原文字符并填入精美的高棉语翻译：

  1. 在受支持的漫画网站（例如 MangaKatana）打开漫画章节。
  2. 点击扩展图标，您将看到 "Manga Detected!"（检测到漫画） 卡片，展示漫画标题、章节和总页数。
  3. 在设置选项卡中选择您喜欢的 漫画翻译模式，然后点击 "Auto Translate Manga"（自动翻译漫画）。
  4. 扩展将自动处理每一页，擦除原有英文/日文对话框并在原处绘制高棉语翻译。

漫画翻译模式

  • 标准模式 (Normal Mode): 擦除原英文/日文对话，并在气泡框内精准绘制高棉语翻译文本（需要配置并启用后端 Manga Server）。
  • 快速模式 (Fast Mode): 直接加载并展示翻译，无需等待图像擦除处理，提供极速的阅读体验。

Gemini API Key 必填 (AI翻译)

您从 Google AI Studio 获取的个人连接密钥。完全免费，无需绑定信用卡，保障您的翻译请求在您的本地浏览器与 Google Gemini 之间直接且安全地传输。
